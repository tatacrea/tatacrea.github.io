Hi! I'm Kayla, a Political Science MA student at the University of Mannheim. I
seek to study political communication in developing countries and high-stakes
political participation by marginalised groups. To answer these questions, I aim
to use causal inference designs and interpret them using mathematical, or
formal, models.

I'm also (re-)learning econometrics (henceforth 'metrics) on a more fundamental
level. I don't think it interests me in the 'want to pursue research agendas in
it' way, but I like me some good brain workouts. When I do 'metrics, I can just
get in the mindspace and get my hands dirty with problems to plow through. It's
tiring, but ultimately rewarding.

My time in academia is pockmarked with points where I felt 'lost' and had no one
to seek counsel in. Academics on the 'net also seem invincible with the lack of
'failure and confusion posts' on their pages. Through my
[blog posts](https://tatacrea.github.io/year-archive/), I seek to chronicle the
nooks and crannies of my journey and make things seem just that bit less
daunting for those starting out after me. I envision it becoming a rubberducking
archive, a lab notebook, and a heap of scrap paper all in one.

I'm also in the early stages of creating learning aids for classes I've taken,
like [this Shiny app](https://github.com/tatacrea/game-theory-shiny) I made for
Game Theory. Feel free to check them out and let me know what you think!

I also do photography on and off. You can see my photos
[here](https://www.flickr.com/people/195727963@N02/).
